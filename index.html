I believe students understand mathematics best when they have opportunities to explore, question, build, and explain.

I am a preservice secondary mathematics teacher completing an Algebra I teaching residency at Northbrook High School in Spring Branch ISD.

My classroom practice centers on conceptual understanding, inquiry, real-world applications, mathematical communication, and creating accessible learning experiences for diverse learners.
